# Ranger's Apprentice: Episode 1, "The Choosing"
*Fan adaptation of* The Ruins of Gorlan *by John Flanagan. Target runtime ~18 minutes.*

**Structure:** Part One spends a whole ordinary day at Castle Redmont, the day *before* the
Choosing, so we get to know Will, Horace, Jenny, Alyss, George, and the adults. Part Two is
the Choosing itself and the tower climb. The darkness (Morgarath) only arrives in the final
moments, as a sting.

**Script format** (the audio pipeline reads this file directly):
- `## SCENE n: TITLE` starts a scene. The `@set` line names the 3D set and its acoustics.
- `**NAME** *(direction)*: line` = one voice clip.
- `[SFX: …]`, `[AMB: …]`, `[MUSIC: …]` = sound cues. `[BEAT n]` = n seconds of silence for action.
- Plain paragraphs = action (what the animation shows).

## SCENE 1: REDMONT AT DAWN
@set rooftops_dawn | outdoor

[MUSIC: dawn]
[AMB: dawn_birds]

The sun comes up over a green valley: a river, farm fields, a village of thatched roofs, and above it all, on its hill, CASTLE REDMONT, golden in the first light. Banners stir. A rooster crows somewhere below.

**NARRATOR** *(warm, storyteller)*: In the Kingdom of Araluen, there is a fief called Redmont. And in Redmont there is a castle, and in that castle lived five children who had no families of their own.

**NARRATOR**: They were called wards. The Baron raised them, fed them, and in the autumn of their fifteenth year, he would help them choose the rest of their lives.

**NARRATOR**: Tomorrow was that day. But today was just another day. And one of those wards was somewhere he definitely was not supposed to be.

The camera rises up the side of the tallest tower. On the very top of the roof, sitting astride the peak with his legs dangling, is WILL: small, wiry, messy brown hair, a patched green tunic. He's eating an apple and watching the sun come up like he owns the view.

[BEAT 2]

He spots something far off: at the edge of the forest, a small shaggy horse and a rider in a grey-green cloak, moving slowly along the tree line.

**WILL** *(to himself, curious)*: The Ranger. Back again.

The rider stops and seems to look straight up at the tower, half a mile away. Will flinches back behind a chimney. When he peeks out again, the rider is gone. There's only trees.

**WILL** *(uneasy laugh)*: …Can't have seen me. Nobody can see that far.

[SFX: church_bell]

A bell rings below. Will's eyes go wide.

**WILL**: Breakfast!

He tosses the apple core, slides down the slates, swings off a gutter, drops onto a lower roof, and is gone.

## SCENE 2: THE CASTLE KITCHENS
@set kitchen | small_room

[MUSIC: playful]
[AMB: kitchen]

A huge warm kitchen. Copper pots, bread ovens, steam, and COOKS rushing everywhere. In the middle, like a general on a battlefield: MASTER CHUBB, short, round, red-faced, a wooden ladle in his hand like a sword.

At a long table, kneading dough with flour up to her elbows, is JENNY: round-faced, rosy-cheeked, curly auburn hair tied up, humming happily. She's not apprenticed yet, but she's here anyway.

**MASTER CHUBB** *(bellowing)*: Who put salt in the honey cakes? WHO?

**JENNY** *(not looking up)*: Nobody, Master Chubb. That's cinnamon. You asked for cinnamon.

**MASTER CHUBB**: …Did I? *(tastes, grudging)* Hm. Carry on.

Behind them, a small high window creaks open. A hand reaches in toward a cooling tray of honey cakes on the sill.

[SFX: window_creak]

Jenny sees it. Smiles. Doesn't say a word.

The hand closes on a cake. Then a second cake. Then, getting greedy, a third—

**MASTER CHUBB** *(spinning around)*: THIEF!

[SFX: ladle_whack]

The ladle cracks down on the windowsill an inch from Will's fingers. The hand vanishes. Outside, a yelp and a thud.

**MASTER CHUBB**: That boy! That climbing, thieving, grinning little—

**JENNY** *(sweetly)*: I think it was a bird, Master Chubb.

**MASTER CHUBB**: A bird. With fingers.

**JENNY**: A very big bird.

Chubb glares at her. She smiles back, the picture of innocence. He grumbles and stomps away.

Jenny slips out of the back door with a little cloth bundle.

## SCENE 3: BEHIND THE KITCHENS
@set kitchen_yard | outdoor

[AMB: courtyard]

Will is sitting in a pile of hay where he landed, one honey cake in his hand, rubbing his head.

**WILL**: I got one. One out of three. That's… a third. That's not nothing.

**JENNY** *(sitting down beside him)*: You're terrible at stealing.

She opens the bundle: four more honey cakes, still warm.

**WILL** *(delighted)*: Jenny! You're a hero. You're the greatest hero in the whole kingdom.

**JENNY**: I know. *(She takes one herself.)* Are you nervous? About tomorrow?

**WILL** *(mouth full)*: No.

**JENNY**: You're nervous.

**WILL**: …A bit. Are you?

**JENNY**: Not even slightly. Master Chubb's going to take me. He doesn't know it yet, but he is. *(She grins.)* What about you? Still Battleschool?

**WILL**: Still Battleschool.

Jenny looks at him: small, skinny, covered in hay. She doesn't say it. She just nudges a second cake toward him.

**JENNY** *(kindly)*: Better eat both, then.

## SCENE 4: THE TRAINING YARD
@set training_yard | outdoor

[MUSIC: rivalry]
[AMB: training_yard]

The Battleschool yard: a dusty square ringed by wooden fences. CADETS in leather jerkins drill with wooden swords while SIR RODNEY, the Battlemaster, mail shirt, short dark beard, a scar through one eyebrow, barks orders.

**SIR RODNEY**: Shields UP! You're a wall, not a hedge! A hedge can be walked through!

[SFX: wooden_swords]

Leaning on the fence, watching every move hungrily, is HORACE: big for fifteen, broad shoulders, short blond hair, square jaw. He's copying the drill with a stick, under his breath. Cut. Block. Thrust.

Will walks up beside him, still chewing.

**WILL**: You're doing it backwards.

**HORACE** *(not looking)*: Go away, Will.

**WILL**: They block, then cut. You're cutting, then blocking. You'd be dead.

Horace turns. He's a full head taller than Will.

**HORACE**: And you'd know, would you? *(He tosses Will a second stick.)* Go on, then. Show me.

The other cadets haven't noticed. Rodney has. He watches from the corner of his eye.

The two boys square off in the dust.

[MUSIC: duel]

Horace attacks: big, powerful swings. Will doesn't block. He *dodges*: ducks under one, sidesteps the next, rolls through Horace's legs and taps him on the back of the knee with his stick.

**WILL** *(grinning)*: Dead.

Horace goes red. He swings harder. Will dodges again, and again, laughing now. Then he slips in the dust, and Horace's stick cracks against his and knocks it flying.

[SFX: stick_clack]

Horace shoves him. Hard. Will flies backward straight into a horse trough.

[SFX: big_splash]

A couple of cadets laugh. Will sits up in the water, dripping, furious.

**HORACE** *(standing over him)*: That's the difference, Will. You can dance around all you like. But one hit, and you're done. *(quieter, almost to himself)* Tomorrow I'll be a knight. What will you be?

He drops his stick and walks away. But as he passes the fence, he stops and looks at the cadets for a long moment, at their jerkins and their real swords, with something in his face that isn't pride at all. It's want. It's like a starving boy outside a bakery.

At the far side of the yard, Sir Rodney writes something on a slate. Then he looks at the dripping boy in the trough, at how small he is, and shakes his head slightly.

## SCENE 5: THE CASTLE LIBRARY
@set library | small_room

[MUSIC: thoughtful]
[AMB: library]

Tall bookshelves, dust in the sunbeams, a sleepy old cat on a windowsill. GEORGE (thin, dark-haired, ink all over his fingers) paces between the shelves, arguing with an empty chair.

**GEORGE**: …and furthermore, my lord, I'd argue that the scribes need me more than I need them. No. No, that's arrogant. *(He switches sides.)* "Is it arrogant, George, or is it simply true?" Hmm. Fair point, George.

At a table by the window, reading calmly, is ALYSS: tall, slender, long pale-blonde hair, a simple blue dress, back perfectly straight.

**ALYSS** *(without looking up)*: You're losing the argument, George.

**GEORGE**: I'm winning *and* losing. That's what makes it hard.

[SFX: squelchy_footsteps]

Will walks in, soaked from head to toe, leaving a trail of puddles on the stone. The cat looks at him with disgust and leaves.

**ALYSS** *(looking up, eyebrow raised)*: Horace?

**WILL**: Horse trough.

**ALYSS**: So, Horace.

**WILL** *(sitting down with a squelch)*: He just got lucky. I had him. I had him three times.

**GEORGE**: On the one hand, three is more than one. On the other hand, the one is the one that counted.

**WILL**: Thank you, George. Very helpful.

**ALYSS** *(closing her book, gently)*: Will. What will you do if Sir Rodney says no?

**WILL**: He won't.

**ALYSS**: But if he does.

Will looks out of the window toward the training yard.

**WILL** *(quietly)*: My father was a knight. Did you know that? He died at Hackham Heath, fighting Morgarath's army. He was a hero. Everyone knows that's where the knights died.

**GEORGE** *(carefully)*: Did someone… tell you that, Will?

**WILL**: …No. But I know. Why else would I be here? The Baron doesn't take in just anyone.

Alyss and George glance at each other. Alyss reaches across and squeezes his wet hand.

**ALYSS**: Then I hope he'd be proud of you. Whatever happens tomorrow.

**WILL** *(firmly)*: Battleschool. That's what happens tomorrow.

## SCENE 6: THE CASTLE GATE, DUSK
@set castle_gate | outdoor

[MUSIC: mystery]
[AMB: market_evening]

The market below the castle walls is packing up. Lanterns are being lit. Will, dry now, sits on top of the gatehouse wall, watching the crowd as the sun sets.

[SFX: hooves_slow]

Then the crowd goes quiet. A small, shaggy horse walks slowly up the road. On it rides a man in a mottled grey-green cloak, hood up, a longbow over his shoulder. HALT.

As he passes, people step back. A mother pulls her little boy behind her skirts. Two farmers make a quick sign against bad luck.

**FARMER 1** *(whispering)*: Ranger.

**FARMER 2** *(whispering)*: They say he can walk through a forest and not move a single leaf. That he talks to crows.

**FARMER 1**: They say he's not even really there. Just a shadow with a bow.

The Ranger rides through the gate directly below Will. Will leans over the edge to get a better look at the man's face, just the edge of a grey beard under the hood—

—and without stopping, without even raising his head, the Ranger speaks.

**HALT** *(low, dry)*: Mind you don't fall, boy.

Will jerks back so fast he nearly does fall. When he looks again, the Ranger is already gone into the shadows of the courtyard.

**WILL** *(whispering)*: How does he *do* that?

## SCENE 7: THE WARDS' DORMITORY, NIGHT
@set dormitory | small_room

[MUSIC: night]
[AMB: night_crickets]

Moonlight through a small window. Four beds. George is asleep, muttering arguments in his sleep. Horace lies on his back, staring at the ceiling. Will sits on the windowsill, knees up, looking at the dark forest.

**GEORGE** *(asleep)*: …objection… sustained…

[BEAT 2]

**HORACE** *(quietly, into the dark)*: Will.

**WILL**: What.

**HORACE**: …The trough. Earlier. *(beat)* That was… you were quick. Today. Quicker than the cadets.

Will turns, surprised.

**WILL**: Was that almost a compliment?

**HORACE**: No. *(beat)* Go to sleep.

[BEAT 2]

**WILL**: Horace? Are you scared? About tomorrow?

A long silence.

**HORACE** *(very quietly)*: Battleschool's all I've got. If they don't take me… there's nothing else I'm good at.

Will doesn't know what to say. For a moment, they're not rivals. Just two orphan boys in the dark.

**WILL**: They'll take you. Look at you. You're the size of a door.

Horace snorts a laugh in the dark. Rolls over.

**HORACE**: Night, Will.

**WILL**: Night.

Will looks out at the forest again. Pulls a small carved wooden token from his shirt: a little shield shape, the only thing he's ever owned that was his before Redmont.

**WILL** *(whispering to the token)*: Tomorrow.

[MUSIC: transition_title]

The window fills the screen. The moonlight becomes a sunrise. The sunrise becomes:

**RANGER'S APPRENTICE**
*Episode One: The Choosing*

## SCENE 8: OUTSIDE THE BARON'S OFFICE, MORNING
@set corridor | stone_hall

[MUSIC: nervous]
[AMB: castle_busy]

Five wards on a long bench outside a heavy oak door, scrubbed and combed and wearing their best clothes. Jenny is bouncing. George is muttering. Alyss is still. Horace is trying very hard to look relaxed. Will's knee won't stop jiggling.

**JENNY**: I didn't eat breakfast. I *couldn't*. That's never happened before. I think I might be dying.

**GEORGE**: You're not dying. You're nervous. Although, technically, everyone is dying, a little, all the time—

**ALYSS**: George.

**GEORGE**: Sorry.

The door opens. A CLERK steps out.

**CLERK**: The wards of Redmont. The Baron will see you now.

They stand. Will catches Horace's eye. Horace gives him the smallest nod. Will nods back.

## SCENE 9: THE BARON'S GREAT HALL
@set great_hall | great_hall

[MUSIC: ceremony]
[AMB: hall_murmur]

A long hall with banners and tall windows. At the far end, behind a great carved table, sits BARON ARALD: enormous, bearded, in deep red robes, with a smile that's mostly kind. Beside him stand the CRAFTMASTERS in a row:
- SIR RODNEY, Battlemaster
- LADY PAULINE, Diplomatic Service: silver hair, silver-grey gown, elegant and still
- MASTER CHUBB, ladle in his belt
- MASTER NIGEL, Chief Scribe: tall, thin, ink-stained, spectacles on a chain
- the HORSEMASTER and the FARMMASTER

And in the shadow of a pillar, almost invisible, a man in a grey-green cloak. Halt. Will sees him. Halt does not appear to see anybody.

**BARON ARALD** *(booming, cheerful)*: Good morning! Five wards, fifteen years old, one very important day, and one very important cook who's missing his breakfast baking to be here, so let's be quick about it.

**MASTER CHUBB** *(grumbling)*: Bread waits for no baron, my lord.

**BARON ARALD**: Quite right. *(He lifts a list.)* When I call your name, step forward and tell us which craft you'd like. Your masters will decide if you'll suit them. Horace Altman.

Horace marches forward, shoulders back.

**HORACE**: Battleschool, my lord.

Sir Rodney walks a slow circle around him. Squeezes his upper arm. Looks at his hands.

**SIR RODNEY**: Strong. Good reach. Needs discipline. *(beat)* We'll take him.

[SFX: soft_crowd_murmur]

Horace's whole body sags with relief. He tries not to grin, and fails completely.

**BARON ARALD**: Alyss Mainwaring.

Alyss steps forward and curtsies perfectly.

**ALYSS**: The Diplomatic Service, my lord. If Lady Pauline will have me.

**LADY PAULINE** *(a small smile)*: She came in asking rather than demanding. That's the first lesson learned already. Yes.

**BARON ARALD**: Jenny.

**JENNY** *(blurting)*: The kitchens! Please! Sorry. The kitchens, my lord, I'd be ever so—

**MASTER CHUBB**: Can you make a sauce without lumps?

**JENNY**: I can make a sauce without lumps, in the dark, with one hand. And I can tell cinnamon from salt.

Chubb's eyes narrow. The Baron hides a smile.

**MASTER CHUBB** *(grudging)*: …Hm. Yes. Fine. Yes.

**BARON ARALD**: George Carter.

**GEORGE**: Scribeschool, my lord, for three reasons. First, my handwriting. Second, I've read every law book in the castle. Third, well, third is really an extension of the second, but I'd argue—

**MASTER NIGEL** *(dryly)*: He argues. Wonderful. Yes. Please. Before he starts again.

[SFX: soft_crowd_laugh]

**BARON ARALD** *(chuckling, then softer)*: And Will.

[MUSIC: stop]

Will steps forward. In this huge hall, he looks even smaller.

**WILL**: Battleschool, my lord.

[BEAT 2]

Sir Rodney walks the same slow circle. He doesn't squeeze Will's arm.

**SIR RODNEY**: You're very small, lad.

**WILL**: I'm fast. I'm the best climber in the castle. Ask anyone. I can—

**SIR RODNEY**: It isn't about climbing. It's about holding a line of shields while men twice your size try to break it. *(not unkindly)* I'm sorry. No.

**WILL** *(voice cracking)*: Please, sir. I'll train harder than anyone. My father was a knight. He died at Hackham Heath. He'd want me to—

Something flickers across the Baron's face. In the shadow of the pillar, Halt's head turns, very slightly.

**SIR RODNEY** *(firmly)*: No.

The hall is silent. Horace looks at the floor.

**BARON ARALD** *(gently)*: Is there another craft you'd consider, Will? The Horsemaster, perhaps—

**WILL** *(barely audible)*: No, my lord.

The Baron sighs and looks at his list. And then Halt steps out of the shadow. He crosses the hall without a sound. People move aside without meaning to.

He places a folded piece of paper on the table in front of the Baron. Says nothing. Steps back into the shadows.

The Baron unfolds it. Reads. His eyebrows go up. He reads it again. Then he looks at Will for a long, long moment.

**BARON ARALD**: Will. I'll give you my decision tomorrow morning.

**WILL**: My lord, I—

**BARON ARALD**: Tomorrow.

He folds the paper and tucks it into a leather folder on the table.

Close on Will's eyes, following that paper.

## SCENE 10: THE CASTLE WALLS, SUNSET
@set walls_sunset | outdoor

[MUSIC: sad]
[AMB: wind_evening]

Will sits alone on top of the outer wall, legs dangling over a long drop. Below, in the courtyard, the others are already living their new lives: Horace being fitted for a training jerkin, laughing with the cadets; Jenny hurrying past with a tray; Alyss walking beside Lady Pauline, copying the way she moves.

**NARRATOR**: By evening, every one of them had somewhere to belong. Everyone except Will.

**WILL** *(bitter, to himself)*: "Tomorrow." The farm. That's what tomorrow is. Pig keeper of Redmont.

He looks up at the tallest tower of the keep. One window catches the last orange light: the Baron's office.

**WILL**: What did you write, Ranger?

He studies the tower. The ivy. The ledges. The gaps between the stones. You can see him measuring it, hold by hold.

**WILL** *(quiet, decided)*: Best climber in the castle.

[MUSIC: tension_build]

## SCENE 11: THE TOWER, NIGHT
@set tower_night | outdoor

[AMB: night_wind]

The moon is half-hidden by clouds. A small shadow crosses the courtyard roofs, barefoot, moving from shadow to shadow. Will.

He reaches the base of the tower. It rises five stories into the dark. He looks up. Swallows. Wipes his palms on his shirt. Starts to climb.

[SFX: climbing_stone]

His fingers wedge into a crack. His toes find a ledge no wider than a coin. He reaches the ivy, tests it, and it holds. He climbs faster.

[BEAT 3]

Halfway up, a gust of wind. The ivy tears away under his hand. He swings out over the drop, hanging by one arm.

[SFX: ivy_rip]
[MUSIC: stop]

His feet scrabble. Find stone. He presses his face against the wall, breathing hard.

**WILL** *(whispering)*: Don't look down. Don't look down. *(He looks down.)* Oh, that was a mistake.

Far below, a GUARD with a lantern walks his round. He stops directly underneath Will. Yawns. Scratches. Looks left. Looks right. Doesn't look up. Walks on.

[MUSIC: tension_build]

Will climbs the last stretch. The window ledge. He hooks his fingers over it, hauls himself up, and eases the shutter open.

[SFX: shutter_creak]

## SCENE 12: THE BARON'S OFFICE
@set office_dark | small_room

[MUSIC: held_breath]
[AMB: clock_tick]

Darkness, except for one stripe of moonlight across a huge desk. Will drops silently onto the floor.

The leather folder is right there in the moonlight, as if someone left it out for him.

He opens it. Unfolds the paper. Tilts it to the light. His hands are shaking.

It's blank. Not a word on it.

**WILL** *(confused whisper)*: …What?

From the darkest corner of the room, from a chair that has been there the whole time:

**HALT** *(low, calm)*: It took you longer than I thought it would.

[SFX: sting]

Will spins around. The paper falls from his fingers. A hooded figure rises out of the dark. Moonlight touches the edge of a grey beard and two very steady eyes.

**WILL**: I— I wasn't— I was just—

**HALT**: Climbing five stories up the outside of the Baron's tower. At night. To read a letter that isn't yours. *(beat)* Yes. I saw.

**WILL**: You were watching me?

**HALT**: I was waiting for you. There's a difference.

He picks up the blank paper, folds it neatly, and slips it into his cloak.

**HALT** *(dry)*: The real one is somewhere safer. I'd assumed somebody might try something foolish tonight.

Will's shoulders drop.

**WILL** *(small)*: What happens now?

**HALT**: Now you go back down. By the stairs, this time. *(He opens the door; torchlight spills in.)* And in the morning, you'll stand in front of the Baron and tell him exactly what you did.

**WILL**: He'll throw me out. He'll send me to the farm.

**HALT**: Perhaps. *(beat)* Perhaps not.

Will walks to the door. Stops. Turns back.

**WILL**: How did you know I'd come?

Halt sits back down in the dark chair. Only his eyes catch the light.

**HALT**: Because it's exactly what I would have done.

[MUSIC: theme_quiet]

Will stares at him. Then he slips out. The door closes.

In the dark, very faintly, there's a sound that might be a quiet laugh.

## SCENE 13: THE MOUNTAINS OF RAIN AND NIGHT
@set morgarath | cavern

[MUSIC: villain]
[AMB: storm]

Far to the east. Black cliffs under endless rain. Lightning shows a jagged plateau and, below it, hundreds of hunched, shaggy shapes moving like a dark tide.

**NARRATOR** *(darker)*: Far to the east, beyond the cliffs where it always rains and the night never really ends, someone else was awake that night.

On a stone balcony stands a tall figure in black: MORGARATH. Pale hair. Pale face. Eyes like ice.

**MORGARATH** *(soft, almost tender)*: Fifteen years. They've forgotten me down there. They hold their festivals. They choose their apprentices. They sleep.

[SFX: wargal_growl]

**MORGARATH**: Let them sleep. *(He turns.)* Sleepers never see the knife.

Behind him, something huge shifts in the darkness. Two dull, unblinking eyes open.

[SFX: beast_snort]
[MUSIC: final_hit]

Black.
