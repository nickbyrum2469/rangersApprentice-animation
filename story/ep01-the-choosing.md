# Ranger's Apprentice: Episode 1, "The Choosing"
*Fan adaptation of* The Ruins of Gorlan *by John Flanagan. Target runtime ~15 minutes.*

**Script format** (kept consistent so it can drive voice recording and animation timing):
- `## SCENE` = new location. `ACTION` lines describe what we see.
- `**NAME** *(direction)*: line` = spoken dialogue. One line = one voice clip.
- `[SFX: …]` = sound effect, `[MUSIC: …]` = music cue, `[CUT]`/`[FADE]` = transitions.

---

## COLD OPEN: THE MOUNTAINS OF RAIN AND NIGHT, NIGHT

[MUSIC: low drone, distant war drums]
[SFX: endless rain, thunder rolling across stone]

ACTION: Black cliffs. Rain falls sideways. Lightning shows a jagged plateau ringed by sheer walls. Hundreds of hunched, shaggy shapes, the WARGALS, move below like a dark tide.

**NARRATOR** *(calm, weighty)*: Fifteen years ago, a baron named Morgarath tried to take the Kingdom of Araluen by force.

ACTION: A flash of memory, painted in red and gold: armies clashing on an open plain. Banners fall.

**NARRATOR**: He was beaten at Hackham Heath and driven into the mountains to the east. A place where it always rains, and the night never really ends.

ACTION: On a stone balcony, a tall figure in black stands motionless in the storm: MORGARATH. Pale hair, pale face, eyes like ice.

**MORGARATH** *(quiet, almost tender)*: Fifteen years. They've forgotten me down there. They hold their festivals. They choose their apprentices. They sleep.

[SFX: a Wargal growl, close]

ACTION: A WARGAL captain crouches at the doorway, snout low.

**MORGARATH**: Let them sleep. *(He turns.)* Sleepers never see the knife.

ACTION: Behind him, something huge shifts in the dark. We only see a massive shadow and two dull, unblinking eyes. Then black.

[SFX: a deep, inhuman snort]
[MUSIC: a single heavy hit]

[CUT TO BLACK]

## SCENE 1: CASTLE REDMONT, WARDS' DORMITORY, NIGHT

[MUSIC: soft, wistful]
[SFX: crickets, a distant owl, wind against old windows]

ACTION: A small room high in the castle. Four beds. Three sleepers. The fourth bed is empty.

ACTION: WILL (15, small, wiry, a mop of brown hair) sits on the stone windowsill with his knees up, looking out over the moonlit fief: fields, a river, the dark line of the forest.

**NARRATOR**: Castle Redmont took in children who had no one else. They were called wards. And tomorrow, the five wards of Redmont would turn fifteen together and be chosen.

**NARRATOR**: Apprenticed to a craftmaster. A trade. A future. A life.

ACTION: Will takes a worn wooden token from his shirt: a carved shield shape, nothing special. He turns it over in his fingers.

**WILL** *(whispering to himself)*: Battleschool. It has to be Battleschool.

ACTION: [ADDED] His imagination takes over. The window becomes a painting in gold light: a KNIGHT on a white horse, sword raised, charging alone into an army.

**WILL** *(V.O., quiet)*: Everyone says I don't remember him. But I know. My father was a knight. He died a hero. He'd want me to be one too.

ACTION: The painted knight fades. Just the dark forest again.

ACTION: One edge of the trees: something moves. A shape in a grey cloak, there and then not. Will blinks. Nothing.

**HORACE** *(from a bed, mumbling)*: Will. Go to sleep. You're breathing too loud.

**WILL**: I'm not even breathing.

**HORACE**: Then stop doing that too.

ACTION: Will slides down from the window and lies on his bed, eyes open. The moonlight crawls across the floor.

[FADE TO]

---

## SCENE 2: CORRIDOR OUTSIDE THE BARON'S OFFICE, MORNING

[MUSIC: bright, nervous strings]
[SFX: footsteps on stone, a busy castle waking up]

ACTION: Sunlight through tall windows. Five teenagers on a long wooden bench outside a heavy oak door.

ACTION: Introduce them one by one as the camera slides along the bench:
- HORACE: big, broad-shouldered, very sure of himself.
- ALYSS: tall, straight-backed, hands folded, perfectly calm.
- JENNY: round-faced, cheerful, can't sit still.
- GEORGE: thin, ink on his fingers, muttering arguments to himself.
- WILL: at the end, smallest, knee bouncing.

**JENNY**: Do you think they'll have food in there? I didn't eat. I couldn't eat. That's never happened before.

**GEORGE** *(to himself)*: On the one hand, scribeschool is clearly the logical choice. On the other hand, the logical choice is exactly what they'll expect, which means—

**ALYSS**: George. Breathe.

**GEORGE**: I am breathing. I'm breathing persuasively.

**HORACE** *(leaning toward Will, grinning)*: Still going to ask for Battleschool, Will?

**WILL**: Yes.

**HORACE**: They'll need a smaller sword. Maybe a butter knife.

**JENNY**: Horace.

**HORACE**: What? I'm being helpful. Sir Rodney takes one look at him and—

**WILL** *(sharp)*: And what?

ACTION: A beat. Horace shrugs, but the smile is meaner now.

**HORACE**: And you'll be shoveling stables by lunch.

ACTION: The oak door swings open. A CLERK appears.

**CLERK**: The wards of Redmont. The Baron will see you now.

ACTION: All five stand. Will's legs are shaking. He makes them stop.

---

## SCENE 3: THE BARON'S HALL, CONTINUOUS

[MUSIC: ceremonial, a little pompous]
[SFX: a hall full of quiet murmuring, then silence]

ACTION: A long hall with banners. At the far end, behind a great table, BARON ARALD: huge, bearded, with a smile that's mostly kind. Beside him, standing in a row: the CRAFTMASTERS.
- SIR RODNEY, Battlemaster, in a mail shirt, arms folded.
- LADY PAULINE, Diplomatic Service, silver-haired, elegant.
- MASTER CHUBB, head cook, a wooden ladle tucked in his belt like a sword.
- MASTER NIGEL, Chief Scribe, ink-stained, spectacles.
- Others: Horsemaster, Farmer, Armorer.

ACTION: And in the shadow of a pillar, almost invisible, a figure in a mottled grey-green cloak, hood up. HALT. Nobody looks at him. Will does, for just a second, then looks away.

**BARON ARALD** *(booming)*: Good morning! Five wards, fifteen years old, one very important day, and *(he gestures at Chubb)* one very important cook who's missing breakfast to be here, so let's be quick.

**MASTER CHUBB** *(grumbling)*: Bread's rising. Bread waits for no baron.

**BARON ARALD**: Indeed. *(He picks up a list.)* When I call your name, step forward and tell us which craft you'd like. Your masters will decide if you'll suit them. Horace Altman.

ACTION: Horace marches forward like he's already a knight.

**HORACE**: Battleschool, my lord.

**BARON ARALD**: Sir Rodney?

ACTION: Rodney walks around Horace slowly, looking him over like a horse at market. Squeezes his shoulder. Nods.

**SIR RODNEY**: Strong. Good reach. Needs discipline. *(beat)* We'll take him.

**HORACE** *(grinning, a glance back at Will)*: Thank you, sir.

**BARON ARALD**: Alyss Mainwaring.

ACTION: Alyss steps forward and curtsies.

**ALYSS**: The Diplomatic Service, my lord. If Lady Pauline will have me.

**LADY PAULINE** *(a small smile)*: She came in asking rather than demanding. That's the first lesson already learned. Yes.

**BARON ARALD**: Jenny.

**JENNY** *(blurting)*: Kitchens! Please! Sorry. The kitchens, my lord, I'd be ever so—

**MASTER CHUBB** *(pointing his ladle)*: Can you make a sauce without lumps?

**JENNY**: I can make a sauce without lumps in the dark with one hand.

**MASTER CHUBB** *(grudging)*: …Hm. We'll see. Yes.

**BARON ARALD**: George Carter.

ACTION: George steps forward, opens his mouth, and the argument just pours out.

**GEORGE**: Scribeschool, my lord, for three reasons. First, my handwriting. Second, I've read every law book in the castle library. Third— well, third is really an extension of the second, but I'd argue—

**MASTER NIGEL** *(dryly)*: He argues. Wonderful. We need someone who argues. Yes.

**BARON ARALD** *(chuckling, then looking down at the list; softer)*: And Will.

[MUSIC: drops to a single sustained note]

ACTION: Will steps forward. In this huge hall, he looks even smaller.

**WILL**: Battleschool, my lord.

ACTION: Quiet. Horace smirks. Sir Rodney's expression doesn't change.

**SIR RODNEY**: Come here, lad.

ACTION: Rodney walks around him once, the same way he did with Horace. He doesn't squeeze his shoulder.

**SIR RODNEY**: You're small.

**WILL**: I'm fast. I'm the best climber in the castle. Ask anyone. I can—

**SIR RODNEY**: It isn't about climbing. It's about holding a line of shields while men twice your weight try to break it. *(not unkind)* I'm sorry. No.

**WILL** *(voice cracking)*: Please, sir. I'll train harder than anyone. I'll— my father was a knight. He died at Hackham Heath. He'd want—

ACTION: Something flickers across the Baron's face. In the shadows, Halt's head turns slightly.

**SIR RODNEY** *(firmly)*: No.

ACTION: Will stands frozen. The hall is silent.

**BARON ARALD** *(gently)*: Is there another craft you'd consider, Will? The Horsemaster perhaps, or—

**WILL** *(barely audible)*: No, my lord.

ACTION: The Baron sighs and looks down at the list. That's when HALT steps out from the pillar. He walks to the table without a sound. People step aside without meaning to.

ACTION: He hands the Baron a folded piece of paper. Says nothing. Steps back into the shadow.

ACTION: The Baron unfolds it. Reads. His eyebrows rise. He reads it again. He looks at Will for a long moment.

**BARON ARALD**: Will. I'll give you my decision tomorrow morning.

**WILL**: My lord, I—

**BARON ARALD**: Tomorrow.

ACTION: He folds the paper and tucks it into a leather folder on the desk.

ACTION: Close on Will's eyes, following the paper.

[CUT]

## SCENE 4: THE CASTLE WALLS, SUNSET

[MUSIC: sad, solo cello]
[SFX: wind, distant laughter from the courtyard]

ACTION: Will sits alone on top of the outer wall, legs hanging over a fatal drop. Below, in the courtyard, Horace is already being fitted for a training jerkin, laughing with the cadets. Jenny runs past with a tray. Alyss walks beside Lady Pauline.

**NARRATOR**: Every one of them had somewhere to go. Everyone except Will.

**WILL** *(to himself, bitter)*: "Tomorrow." What's tomorrow? The farm? Pig keeper?

ACTION: He looks up at the tallest tower of the keep, where the Baron's office window catches the last orange light.

**WILL**: What did you write, Ranger?

ACTION: He thinks of Halt: the cloak, the silence, the way people moved aside.

**WILL**: Everyone's scared of Rangers. They say they do black magic. That they can turn invisible.

ACTION: He looks at the tower again. Measures it with his eyes. The ivy. The ledges. The gaps between the stones.

**WILL** *(quietly, decided)*: I'm the best climber in the castle.

[MUSIC: the cello becomes a quiet, ticking pulse]

[FADE TO]

---

## SCENE 5: THE KEEP TOWER, EXTERIOR, NIGHT

[MUSIC: tense, building, pizzicato strings]
[SFX: wind gusting, ivy rustling, a dog barking far below]

ACTION: The moon is half hidden. A small dark figure crosses the courtyard roofs: Will, barefoot, moving from shadow to shadow.

ACTION: He reaches the base of the tower. It rises up and up into the dark, five stories. He looks up. Swallows. Wipes his hands on his shirt.

ACTION: He starts to climb.

[SFX: fingers on stone, a scrape, breathing]

ACTION: Montage of the climb:
- His fingers wedge into a crack. His toes find a ledge the width of a coin.
- He reaches the ivy and tests it. It holds. He climbs faster.
- Halfway: a gust of wind. The ivy rips loose under one hand. He swings out over the drop, hanging by one arm.

[SFX: ripping vines, a gasp]
[MUSIC: stops dead]

ACTION: Silence. His feet scrabble. Find stone. He presses his face against the wall, breathing hard.

**WILL** *(whispering)*: Don't look down. Don't look down. *(He looks down.)* Oh, that was a mistake.

ACTION: Far below, a GUARD walks his round with a lantern. He stops directly below Will. Yawns. Looks around. Doesn't look up. Walks on.

[MUSIC: the pulse returns]

ACTION: Will keeps climbing. The window ledge appears above him, a dark, deep arch. He hooks his fingers over it and hauls himself up.

ACTION: The window is shuttered but not latched. He eases it open.

[SFX: an old hinge, the faintest creak]

---

## SCENE 6: THE BARON'S OFFICE, CONTINUOUS

[MUSIC: very quiet, high strings, holding a note]
[SFX: Will's breathing, a ticking clock]

ACTION: Pitch dark, except for a stripe of moonlight across a huge desk. Will drops silently onto the floor.

ACTION: He creeps to the desk. The leather folder is right there, in the moonlight, as if someone left it out.

ACTION: He opens it. The folded paper. His hands are shaking. He unfolds it and tilts it to the moonlight.

ACTION: It's blank. Not a word on it.

**WILL** *(confused whisper)*: …What?

ACTION: Behind him, in the darkest corner of the room, something moves. A shape in a chair, which has been there the whole time.

**HALT** *(low, calm, unimpressed)*: It took you longer than I thought it would.

[MUSIC: a sharp sting]

ACTION: Will spins around, the paper falling from his fingers. A hooded figure stands up out of the dark. Moonlight touches the edge of a grey beard and two very steady eyes.

**WILL** *(terrified)*: I— I wasn't— I was just—

**HALT**: Climbing five stories up the outside of the Baron's tower in the middle of the night. To read a letter that isn't yours. *(beat)* Yes. I saw.

**WILL**: You were watching me?

**HALT**: I was waiting for you. There's a difference.

ACTION: Halt steps forward. He picks up the blank paper from the floor, folds it neatly, and slips it back into his cloak.

**WILL**: It was blank. The paper was blank.

**HALT**: The real one is somewhere safer. *(dry)* I'd assumed somebody might try something foolish tonight.

ACTION: Will's shoulders drop. He knows he's finished.

**WILL** *(small voice)*: What happens now?

ACTION: Halt looks at him for a long moment. We can't read his face at all.

**HALT**: Now? Now you go back down. By the stairs, this time. *(He opens the door; torchlight from the corridor spills in.)* And in the morning, you'll stand in front of the Baron and tell him exactly what you did.

**WILL**: He'll throw me out. He'll send me to the farm. Or worse.

**HALT**: Perhaps. *(beat)* Perhaps not.

ACTION: Will walks past him to the door. He stops. Turns back.

**WILL**: Why were you waiting for me? How did you even know I'd come?

ACTION: Halt sits back down in the dark chair. Only his eyes catch the light.

**HALT**: Because it's what I would have done.

[MUSIC: the main theme, slow and quiet, on a single flute]

ACTION: Will stares at him, then slips out. The door closes. Darkness.

ACTION: In the dark, the faintest sound. It might be a quiet laugh.

[FADE TO BLACK]

### Production notes for this episode
- **Spoken words:** ~780 (dialogue + narration), about 5–6 minutes of speech. With the action beats, the climb sequence, the title and the music, that's an estimated **10–12 minutes**. To reach a full 15, the dormitory, corridor and walls scenes can each take a longer exchange.
- **Characters with lines:** Narrator, Morgarath, Will, Horace, Alyss, Jenny, George, Baron Arald, Sir Rodney, Lady Pauline, Master Chubb, Master Nigel, Halt, Clerk.
- **Locations:** Morgarath's plateau, dormitory, corridor, great hall, castle walls, tower exterior, Baron's office.
- **Book differences:** the blank paper and the "It's what I would have done" exchange are this
  adaptation's own staging of the office scene. Episode 2 reveals what Halt's real note said.
