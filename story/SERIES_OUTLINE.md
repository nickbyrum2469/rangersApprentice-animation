# Ranger's Apprentice: Book 1, *The Ruins of Gorlan*
## Full series outline (fan adaptation)

The whole book adapted as **10 episodes of about 15 minutes each** (about 2.5 hours total).
Every major event from the book is here, in order. Additions that aren't in the book are
marked **[ADDED]**. They're small scenes that help the story play on screen.

> Written from memory of the book. If any detail is off, mark it and I'll fix the
> outline before we lock that episode's script. All dialogue is written fresh for this
> adaptation, not copied from the book.

## Episode 1: "The Choosing"
*Full script written: [`ep01-the-choosing.md`](ep01-the-choosing.md)*

1. **Prologue:** in the Mountains of Rain and Night, Morgarath broods over his defeat at Hackham Heath fifteen years ago and plans his return. Wargals howl below.
2. Night before Choosing Day. Will can't sleep. He looks out at the castle and remembers the only thing he knows about his father: that he died a hero. **[ADDED: a short "imagined father" vision of a knight in battle.]**
3. Choosing Day morning. The five wards (Will, Horace, Alyss, Jenny, George) wait outside the Baron's office. Horace needles Will about his size.
4. The Choosing: each ward steps up. Horace goes to Battleschool, Alyss to the Diplomatic Service, Jenny to the kitchens, George to Scribeschool.
5. Will asks for Battleschool. Sir Rodney turns him down: too small. Will begs and is refused again.
6. Halt, silent in the corner, hands the Baron a folded paper. The Baron says Will's fate will be decided tomorrow.
7. That night, Will decides he has to know what the paper says. He climbs the outside of the tower to the Baron's office window.
8. Inside, in the dark, he finds the paper on the desk. Then a voice from the shadows: Halt has been waiting for him. **Cliffhanger.**

## Episode 2: "The Ranger's Apprentice"
1. Morning: Will is brought before the Baron, expecting punishment.
2. Reveal: the paper was Halt's request to take Will as his apprentice. Halt explains he'd guessed Will would try the climb, and that the climb proved his point.
3. Will is offered the choice: Ranger apprentice, or the farm. Will wishes it were Battleschool, but chooses the Ranger.
4. Will packs his few things and follows Halt to his cabin at the edge of the forest. Halt barely speaks.
5. First days: chores, firewood, cleaning, being watched. Will thinks Halt is wasting his time.
6. Meanwhile at Battleschool: Horace's first day. He's strong but clumsy. Three senior cadets, **Alda, Bryn and Jerome**, single him out.
7. Halt gives Will his first real test and quietly approves. End with Halt handing him a mottled grey-green **Ranger cloak**.

## Episode 3: "Bow and Knife"
1. Training montage: Will's first bow. He's terrible, then slowly isn't.
2. The **saxe knife** and **throwing knife**: Halt teaches him to throw. Missing, missing, then a hit.
3. **Unseen movement**: how Rangers stay hidden in plain sight. A game where Will has to sneak up on Halt (and fails).
4. Halt explains what Rangers really are: the King's eyes and ears, and why people fear them.
5. Battleschool: Horace's bullying gets worse, at night, in secret. He's too proud to tell anyone.
6. **[ADDED]** Will and Horace pass each other in the castle. Old resentment. Neither knows what the other is going through.

## Episode 4: "Tug"
1. Halt and Will ride (Will on foot, struggling) to **Old Bob's** farm in the forest.
2. Will chooses a scruffy, stubborn little horse: **Tug**.
3. The secret: Ranger horses are trained not to let anyone ride them without a special phrase. Will finds out by getting thrown, repeatedly.
4. Old Bob reveals the phrase. Will rides, and it's the first time he feels like a Ranger.
5. Riding lessons; the bond between Will and Tug.

## Episode 5: "Harvest Day"
1. Harvest Day festival at Redmont: stalls, food, music. Will has his first wages and buys gifts.
2. Will meets Jenny (thriving in the kitchens), George (debating) and Alyss (now poised and diplomatic). They've all grown.
3. Will runs into Horace. Old tension flares and they fight.
4. **Halt** steps in and breaks it up, and sees more than either boy realizes.

## Episode 6: "Three Against One"
1. Halt sees the bruises Horace has been hiding and works out what Alda, Bryn and Jerome have been doing.
2. Halt confronts the three bullies and arranges a fair fight. Horace faces them and wins.
3. Will and Horace finally talk honestly and become friends.
4. Sir Rodney learns what happened; the bullies are thrown out of Battleschool.
5. Horace finally thrives.

## Episode 7: "The Gathering"
1. Halt and Will travel to the yearly **Ranger Gathering**.
2. Will meets **Gilan** (Halt's former apprentice) and **Crowley**, the Ranger Commandant.
3. Will's apprentice assessment: tracking, archery, unseen movement.
4. Will passes and receives his **bronze oakleaf**, the sign of a Ranger apprentice.
5. **[ADDED]** A campfire scene where Gilan tells stories about Halt that Halt would rather he didn't.

## Episode 8: "News from the Mountains"
1. Dark news arrives: Morgarath has unleashed the **Kalkara**, two huge, ape-like beasts whose stare can paralyze. They've already killed an important lord of the kingdom.
2. Crowley's council: the Kalkara must be stopped before Morgarath uses them on the King's commanders.
3. Halt, Gilan and Will are sent to hunt them.
4. The tracking begins. Signs of the beasts, unnerving and huge. Will learns real fear.

## Episode 9: "The Ruins of Gorlan"
1. The trail leads toward the **Ruins of Gorlan**, Morgarath's old castle.
2. Halt sends Gilan riding for help: Baron Arald and Sir Rodney. Horace insists on coming with them.
3. Halt and Will continue alone. Night falls in the ruins.
4. The Kalkara attack. Arrows barely slow them. Halt is hurt.

## Episode 10: "Fire in the Dark"
1. Baron Arald, Sir Rodney and Horace arrive. Battle with the first Kalkara; the Baron is badly injured.
2. Will remembers what the Kalkara are coated in. He makes a fire arrow and sets the second beast ablaze.
3. Aftermath: Halt and the Baron recover.
4. A ceremony at the King's court honors Will and Horace. Will is offered a place at Battleschool and turns it down: he's a Ranger.
5. Halt tells Will the truth about his father. He was a soldier who died bravely at Hackham Heath, and Halt brought Will to Redmont as a baby.
6. Final shot: Will and Halt riding back into the forest. "End of Book One."
